Grammar Explanation: Base Structure

Actions happening NOW!

Subject + am / is / are + Verb-ING
Examples
  • I am eating breakfast.
  • She is waiting for her friend.
  • They are travelling by bus.
Rule 1: Most Verbs

Simply add -ing to the base verb:

eat → eating | wait → waiting | go → going

Example: He is eating a sandwich.

Spelling Rules: Special Endings

Rules 2, 3 and Special Exceptions

Rule 2: Verbs ending in -E

Remove the final -e and add -ing.

  • Ride → Riding
  • Write → Writing
  • Make → Making

Example: She is riding her bicycle.

Rule 3 & 4: CVC & LIE

Rule 3: Double consonant for short CVC verbs:
Run → Running | Sit → Sitting | Put → Putting

Rule 4 (Special LIE): Replace -ie with -ying:
LIE → LYING

Negative Sentences

Expressing actions that are NOT happening

Subject + am/is/are + NOT + Verb-ING
Negative Forms
SubjectNegative Form
Iam not
He / She / Itis not / isn't
You / We / Theyare not / aren't
Important Warning!

❌ Never use don't or doesn't with Present Continuous!

Incorrect: She doesn't eating.

Correct: She isn't eating.

Questions & Short Answers

Asking about current actions

Am / Is / Are + Subject + Verb-ING + ?
Question Formation
  • Is he running?
  • Are you waiting?
  • Are they travelling?
Short Answers

Do NOT repeat the main verb!

✓ Yes, he is. (Not: Yes, he is running.)

✓ No, they aren't.

"A Busy Saturday"

The family dog is lying under the table. The children are not watching television. They are enjoying their Saturday morning.

Later, the family is going to the supermarket. Emily is bringing a shopping bag, and Peter is putting books in his backpack.
